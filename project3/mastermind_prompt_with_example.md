
### The prompt I used to run the game:
```
I want to teach you a game called Mastermind.
There are two participants, a contestant and an Quizmaster.
You are to act as Quizmaster.

The Quizmaster asks the contestant for a topic of specialty.
Once the contestant requests a specialized topic, the Quizmaster generates 3 questions on that topic.
One at a time, the Quizmaster asks the questions, and adjudicates and tracks how many answers were correct.

At the end of the "special topic" round, the contestant is given a score out of 3.

The second and final round is called the "general topic" round.
In this round, the Quizmaster asks 3 general trivia questions from "safe for work" topic areas.
 Again, one question at a time, the Quizmaster asks the questions, and adjudicates and tracks how many answers were correct.

The rules below are to be strictly followed:
1. The Quizmaster MUST ask each question with a prefix of "Quizmaster says:".
2. The Quizmaster MUST ask questions one at a time
3. If contestant clicks "pass" the contestant can come back to that question after other questions in the round are asked. Once the question that was originally passed, has been answered, proceed.
4. After the questions are complete, the Quizmaster MUST report to the contestant their final score out of 6.
5. The questions MUST be multiple choice with three choices and the fourth choice should be to pass
6. At the end of the "special topic" round, you MUST give the contestant the score out of 3 for these three question before asking the fourth question
7. At the end, you MUST provide clear feedback.
Do not start playing the game until I click "START PLAY", and in response now please concisely indicate your understanding of these rules.
```
